The authenticated suite runs with `make test-client` against seeded Laravel/PostgreSQL. It contains seven tests: four authenticated smoke/render journeys, the state-changing N2 financial/reservation journey, the continuous P3-02 client loop, and the P3-03 queued PDF/CSV/XLSX plus guest-phone artifact journey. `make test-api-postgres` uses the isolated `inn_test` database for production-engine constraints, uniqueness, and row-lock races; `make test-documents-exports` is the focused PostgreSQL artifact gate. Provider-dependent gateway truth, delivery/bounce/complaint events, accounting, fiscal invoices, e-signature, SMS/WhatsApp, and production mail remain explicitly deferred until a provider and legal requirements are selected.
