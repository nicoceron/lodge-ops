The authenticated suite runs with `make test-client` against seeded Laravel/PostgreSQL. It contains seven tests: four authenticated smoke/render journeys, the state-changing N2 financial/reservation journey, the continuous P3-02 client loop, and the P3-03 queued PDF/CSV/XLSX plus guest-phone artifact journey. `make test-api-postgres` uses the isolated `inn_test` database for production-engine constraints, uniqueness, and row-lock races; `make test-documents-exports` is the focused PostgreSQL artifact gate.

P3-06A is now implementation-complete at the deterministic fake/contract/database layer, with the remaining release blocker recorded in the [payment operations runbook](p3-06a-payment-operations-runbook.md): no Mercado Pago credentials or public HTTPS webhook are configured, so the real test-mode state-changing journey and receipts have not been claimed. After that evidence passes, [P3-06B](p3-06b-front-desk-tenders-implementation-plan.md) proves cash/standalone-terminal truth and [P3-06C](p3-06c-mercado-pago-point-qr-implementation-plan.md) proves Point/QR automation. Delivery events, accounting, fiscal invoices, e-signature, SMS/WhatsApp, production mail and production payment activation remain separate gates.
